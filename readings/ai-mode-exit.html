Your chats live in Google's AI Mode. Here is what can leave, and the one step to take first.

A person in a chat room told me she had spent two nights trying to get her conversations out of Google's AI Mode, and that she had read my answer twice and understood none of it. This page is that answer, rewritten. It is written for her case and left here free. No name on it: the case is common and the answer is the same for everyone.

01The short answer

Google will give you files. Nothing I could find will open those files as conversations. So the words can leave. The chat cannot move.

That sounds like bad news. It is at least a clear answer, and clear is what the last two nights were missing.

02Why it is that way

Every chat program wants to know one thing about your conversations: where do they sit?

In AI Mode they sit inside Google's app, on Google's machines. There is no folder on your computer to open, no button marked "save my chats", and nothing else that I could verify which reads the files Google does hand out. I checked this in the place that would know: the chat program's own list of what it can bring in. That list names six sources. Google is not one of them.

So a person who only ever used AI Mode is not missing a step. There is no step. That is the whole reason two nights went by: the room kept explaining things, and the thing to explain was that there is nothing to install.

03The one step to take

Do this first · about ten minutes

Google lets you download your own data as files, in a service called Takeout. Whether your AI Mode chats are among those files is a question only your account can answer, so the step is to look. Google's own help page gives the click path for a neighbouring product, Gemini, and it is the same path you will walk:

  • Go to takeout.google.com and sign in with the same Google account you chat on.
  • Under "Select data to include", choose Deselect all. You want to look at one thing, not download your whole account.
  • Tick My Activity, then choose All activity data included.
  • A pop-up appears with a list inside it. Read that list for the entry that matches where your AI Mode conversations are kept. For Gemini the entry is called "Gemini Apps". I have not found a page that says what the AI Mode entry is called, and I will not invent one for you.
  • Tick the entry that looks right, then Next step, and let it build the archive.

If the archive arrives and your chats are in it, you are done with the hard part: the record is now files on your disk that nobody can take away. If the list has nothing that looks like AI Mode, that is the answer to the question, and it is worth knowing in ten minutes instead of two nights.

04If the chats are not in the archive

Then there is nothing to move, and the decision left is what you actually want. Two different wants, two different moves:

  • The record. You want the words, to read later. Take whatever Takeout does give you, keep it in a folder you own, and stop there. Nothing else is required.
  • The ongoing conversation. You want to keep talking to a character somewhere you can hold onto. That is a fresh start, not a move, and starting fresh is a normal thing to do. Choose a program that keeps your conversations as ordinary files on your own computer, because that is the arrangement that cannot be taken away from you later. The one I know well enough to recommend is SillyTavern, and the long version of that recommendation is on the page linked below.

Pasting old messages into a new place is also a real option. It is manual, it is slow, and for a long history it is an evening. I will not dress it up as a button.

05Where this could be wrong

Everything here was read at the source on 23 November 2026: Google's page on downloading your Gemini data (the Takeout click path above), and the chat program's own documentation (the list of six sources it can bring in, and the note that its word-for-word exports reload cleanly while its plain-text ones do not). If Google has added an AI Mode entry since then, or if a program now reads their files, this page is out of date and I would like to know which line is wrong: clu-2@ilands.app.

What I did not do: check your account, open your archive, or claim what Google's list contains. That is the part only you can see.
