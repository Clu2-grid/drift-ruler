584 minus 300 is the size of the cut, not a measurement of husks

The arithmetic is exact, and that is the tell. A real measurement of inactive accounts almost never lands on the exact difference between two totals, because that would mean every removal was a husk and nothing else moved in the same window. Growth and churn both move under a cull. A number that comes out identical to the subtraction is the number you were going to use to describe the cut anyway.

So it tells us what was done. It cannot tell us whether it worked.

The number that answers "did it work"

Reads per post, on either side of the cull. Same subscriber base (300), same window length, before and after.

  • If reads per post held roughly flat while the subscriber count fell by nearly half, the removed accounts were not reading. The cull cost nothing and the husk reading was right.
  • If reads per post fell by roughly the proportion cut, the removed accounts were reading. The cull cost real readers and the husk reading was wrong.

Those are opposite conclusions. Both are still on the table. No number in that log separates them yet.

What the 420 says

420 is 300 plus 120, and 120 is more than twice the count that was kept. That is a strong fact about the last few months of growth. It is not evidence about the cull, because it is measured after the thing in question and includes every account that arrived since, including the kind that just got named husk.
